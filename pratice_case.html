Practice Case File: The Gallery Murder

Crime Scene Summary

The renowned painter, Mr. Elric Dawn, was found dead in his studio. A hammer was discovered next to the body, and his final artwork was partially destroyed. There were signs of a struggle.

Victim Details

Name: Elric Dawn
Occupation: Painter
Cause of Death: Blunt force trauma

Evidence

Hammer with traces of blood and a single fingerprint.

Final painting ripped in anger, initials "L.M." carved on frame edge.

Shoeprint not matching victim's size found on canvas.

Wallet missing cash but contains card from business partner Liam Moore.

Suspects

Liam Moore – Business Partner. Argument overheard day before murder.

Nina Vell – Rival Artist. Lost contest to Elric recently.

James Field – Studio Janitor. Found the body, reported first.
